============================================================
HOW TO APPLY FOR A SCHOLARSHIP IN ISKOMATS
============================================================

Step 1: Create an Account

• Visit the iskoMats website.
• Click the Register button.
• Enter your personal information.
• Verify your email address.
• Log in to your account.

------------------------------------------------------------

Step 2: Complete Your Profile

Fill in all required information, including:

• Personal Information
• Address
• School Information
• Course
• Year Level
• Academic Information
• Household Income

Make sure all information is accurate before saving your profile.

------------------------------------------------------------

Step 3: Use the Match Scholarship Feature

• Open the "Match Scholarship" page.
• The system will compare your information with the scholarship requirements.
• Eligible scholarships will be displayed.
• Scholarships that do not match your qualifications will also be shown as not eligible.

------------------------------------------------------------

Step 4: Select a Scholarship

Choose the scholarship you want to apply for.

Available Scholarships:

• Mayor Africa Scholarship
• Governor Vilma Scholarship

------------------------------------------------------------

Step 5: Upload the Required Documents

Upload clear and readable copies of all required documents.

Examples include:

• Registration or Enrollment Form
• Certificate of Grades
• Certificate of Indigency or Barangay Residency
• Student ID
• Other scholarship-specific requirements

The system may reject blurry or incomplete documents.

------------------------------------------------------------

Step 6: Submit Your Application

After reviewing all information and uploaded documents:

• Click Submit Application.
• Wait for the confirmation message.

Your application status will become "Pending."

------------------------------------------------------------

Step 7: Wait for Verification

The scholarship administrator will review:

• Your profile information
• Your eligibility
• Your uploaded documents
• OCR verification results
• Face similarity verification (if applicable)

If additional documents are required, you will receive a notification.

------------------------------------------------------------

Step 8: Track Your Application

Go to "My Applications" to monitor your application.

Possible statuses include:

• Pending
• Under Verification
• Approved
• Rejected
• Cancelled

You will also receive notifications through the system and your registered email whenever your application status changes.

============================================================
APPLICATION REMINDERS
============================================================

• Ensure all information entered is true and complete.
• Upload only clear and readable documents.
• Submit all required documents before the deadline.
• Incomplete applications may not be processed.
• Meeting the eligibility requirements does not guarantee approval.
• The final decision is made by the scholarship administrator.

============================================================
COMMON QUESTIONS
============================================================

Q: How do I apply for a scholarship?

A: Register an account, complete your profile, use the Match Scholarship feature, choose a scholarship, upload the required documents, and submit your application.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Can I apply without completing my profile?

A: No. Your profile must be completed before you can apply for any scholarship.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: What happens after I submit my application?

A: Your application will be marked as Pending. The scholarship administrator will verify your documents and review your eligibility before making a final decision.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Can I edit my application after submitting it?

A: No. Once an application has been submitted, you cannot edit it. If corrections are needed, wait for instructions from the scholarship administrator.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Can I apply for another scholarship if I am already approved?

A: No. Once you are officially approved and accept one scholarship, all other scholarship applications will automatically be cancelled because each student may only receive one scholarship through the iskoMats system.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How do I know if I am eligible?

A: Use the Match Scholarship feature. The system compares your profile with the eligibility requirements of each scholarship and displays the scholarships you qualify for.
