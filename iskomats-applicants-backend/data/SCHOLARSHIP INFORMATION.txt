============================================================
SCHOLARSHIP INFORMATION
============================================================

The iskoMats system currently supports two scholarship programs:

1. Mayor Africa Scholarship
2. Governor Vilma Scholarship

============================================================
MAYOR AFRICA SCHOLARSHIP
============================================================

Mayor Africa Scholarship has two scholarship categories:

------------------------------------------------------------
A. Mayor Africa College Scholarship
------------------------------------------------------------

Eligibility Requirements for Mayor Africa College Scholarship

• Must be a resident of Lipa City.
• Must be currently enrolled in college.
• Must have NO grades below 80.
• Must submit all required documents.

Required Documents for Mayor Africa College Scholarship

1. Registration or Enrollment Form.
2. Certified True Copy of Grades.
3. Student ID.

------------------------------------------------------------
B. Mayor Africa College Educational Financial Assistance
------------------------------------------------------------

Eligibility Requirements for Mayor Africa College Educational Financial Assistance

• Must be a resident of Lipa City.
• Must be currently enrolled in college.
• Must have NO failing grade.
• Must submit all required documents.

Required Documents for Mayor Africa College Educational Financial Assistance

1. Registration or Enrollment Form.
2. Grades.
3. Certificate of Barangay Residency.
4. Student ID.

============================================================
GOVERNOR VILMA SCHOLARSHIP
============================================================

Governor Vilma Scholarship - College Educational Financial Assistance

Eligibility Requirements for Governor Vilma Scholarship

• Must be a resident of Lipa City.
• Must be currently enrolled in college.
• Must have NO failing grade.
• Must submit all required documents.

Required Documents for Governor Vilma Scholarship

1. Registration or Enrollment Form.
2. Grades.
3. Certificate of Barangay Residency.
4. Student ID.

============================================================
GENERAL SCHOLARSHIP POLICIES
============================================================

• Only students who are currently residing in Lipa City and studying in Lipa City may apply through iskoMats.

• Students may apply to multiple scholarship programs if they meet the eligibility requirements.

• Once a student is officially approved and accepts one scholarship, all other scholarship applications will automatically be cancelled because each student may only receive one scholarship through the iskoMats system.

• Meeting all eligibility requirements does not automatically guarantee approval. Final approval is made by the scholarship administrator.

============================================================
MERITS AND AWARDS SCORING SYSTEM
============================================================

• Only 1 merit or academic award may be submitted per application.
• Merits provide up to 25 points toward your total ranking score (Total Ranking Score is 100 points max: GPA is up to 75 points, Merit is up to 25 points, and Household Income is 0 points).
• Only official academic honors and achievements backed by an uploaded certificate, diploma, medal, or formal academic proof are accepted.
• Non-academic achievements (sports, pageantry, social clubs, arts/culture, or other non-academic extracurriculars) are NOT accepted for merit scoring.

Calibrated Scoring Scale for Academic Honors (0 to 25 points):
1. Summa Cum Laude – 25 points (Highest Academic Distinction / Valedictorian / National Olympiad Champion)
2. Magna Cum Laude – 23 points (High Academic Distinction / Salutatorian / Regional Olympiad Champion)
3. 1st Honor / First Honor / With Highest Honors – 20 points (Top Class Academic Honor)
4. Cum Laude – 18 points (Academic Distinction)
5. 2nd Honor / Second Honor / With High Honors – 15 points (Second Class Academic Honor)
6. 3rd Honor / Third Honor / With Honors – 10 points (Third Class Academic Honor / Dean's List / School-level Academic Contests)
7. General Academic Recognition / Certificate – 7 points

Tip: When submitting, specify the scope/level of the academic honor (School, Division/City, Regional, National, or International) and make sure to upload the clear supporting certificate.

============================================================
COMMON QUESTIONS
============================================================

Q: Can I apply for both scholarships?

A: Yes. You may apply for both available scholarships if you satisfy their eligibility requirements. However, once you are officially accepted into one scholarship, your remaining scholarship applications will automatically be cancelled.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: I do not live in Lipa City. Can I apply?

A: No. Only students who are currently residing and studying in Lipa City are eligible to apply through iskoMats.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: I have one failing grade. Can I apply?

A:
• Mayor Africa College Educational Financial Assistance – No, applicants must have no failing grades.
• Governor Vilma Educational Financial Assistance – No, applicants must have no failing grades.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: I have a grade below 80. Can I apply for Mayor Africa College Scholarship?

A: No. Applicants for the Mayor Africa College Scholarship must not have any grade below 80.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Does completing all requirements guarantee approval?

A: No. Completing all requirements only means your application can be evaluated. The final decision is made by the scholarship administrator.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: What if I upload incomplete documents?

A: Your application may be marked as incomplete or you may be asked to upload the missing or correct documents before your application can proceed.

------------------------------------------------------------

Q: What type of merits can I input?

A: You can input 1 official academic honor or achievement backed by a supporting certificate, diploma, medal, or formal academic recognition. Merits provide up to 25 points toward your ranking score (GPA is up to 75 points, Merits up to 25 points, Total is 100 points). Non-academic achievements (such as sports, pageantry, social clubs, arts/culture, or non-academic extracurriculars) are not accepted for merit scoring.

Examples of valid academic merits and points:
• Summa Cum Laude / Valedictorian / National Olympiad Champion: 25 points
• Magna Cum Laude / Salutatorian / Regional Olympiad Champion: 23 points
• 1st Honor / First Honor / With Highest Honors: 20 points
• Cum Laude: 18 points
• 2nd Honor / Second Honor / With High Honors: 15 points
• 3rd Honor / Third Honor / With Honors / Dean's List: 10 points
• General Academic Recognition / Certificate: 7 points

Tip: Specify the title and scope/level of the honor (School, Division/City, Regional, National, or International) and upload your clear supporting certificate.
